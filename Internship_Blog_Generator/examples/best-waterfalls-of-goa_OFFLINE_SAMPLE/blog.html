Dudhsagar Falls

RegionDharbandora
Entry feeSanctuary entry approx Rs 50 + jeep safari approx Rs 400-450 per person (full jeep approx Rs 2400-2500)
TimingsJeep safari: 6:00 AM - 5:30 PM; ticket counter closes around 2:00 PM; closed during heavy monsoon (mid-June to September)
Best timeOctober to early June (best flow June-September, but access restricted in peak monsoon)

A spectacular four-tiered waterfall on the Mandovi River at the Goa-Karnataka border, among the tallest waterfalls in India (approx 310 m); reached via jeep safari through Bhagwan Mahavir Wildlife Sanctuary or by trekking along the Castle Rock railway line.

Why Visit

Recommended for Adventure Seekers, Photography, Trekkers. Trek to the falls from Kulem or Castle Rock, or take the forest-department jeep safari; swim (with a life jacket) in the natural pool at the base; birdwatch for hornbills and barbets along the trail.

What Makes It Special

Long a subject of local legend (the name means 'Sea of Milk' from its frothy white cascade); has been a landmark since colonial-era railway surveys of the Braganza Ghats in the 19th century.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit is October to early June (best flow June-September, but access restricted in peak monsoon).

Nearby Attractions

Nearby you can also explore Bhagwan Mahavir Wildlife Sanctuary & Mollem National Park (~3.5 km).

Travel Tip

Wear non-slip footwear; check whether the waterfall/trek is open, since access can close in heavy monsoon; carry water and a change of clothes if swimming.

Kuske Waterfall

RegionSanguem
Entry feeFree
Timings8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Best timeJun-Sept

Kuske Waterfall, also known as Kuskem Waterfall, is a beautiful seasonal waterfall located near Kuskem village in Sanguem Taluka, South Goa. Nestled amidst the lush forests of the Western Ghats, the waterfall is surrounded by rich greenery, rolling hills, and a tranquil natural environment, making it an ideal destination for nature lovers and trekking enthusiasts. During the monsoon season, the waterfall comes alive with a strong flow of crystal-clear water, creating a refreshing and picturesque landscape.

Why Visit

Recommended for Nature Lovers, Trekkers. Trekking, Photography, Sightseeing

What Makes It Special

Kuske Waterfall is a natural waterfall located in the Sanguem region of South Goa, surrounded by the lush greenery of the Western Ghats. It is known for its peaceful environment, rocky landscape, and natural beauty.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit is Jun-Sept.

Travel Tip

Wear sturdy footwear and avoid slippery rocks.

Salaulim Waterfall (Salaulim Dam Spillway)

RegionSanguem
Entry feeFree
Timings8:00 AM - 5:00 PM
Best timeJul-Oct

Salaulim Waterfall, commonly seen during the monsoon at the Salaulim Dam in Sanguem Taluka, South Goa, is a seasonal attraction created when excess water is released through the dam's spillway. Surrounded by green hills, forests, and the scenic reservoir, the waterfall offers breathtaking views and is a popular destination for nature lovers, photographers, and visitors seeking a peaceful getaway. The powerful flow of water during the rainy season creates a spectacular sight and enhances the natural beauty of the area.

Why Visit

Recommended for Nature Lovers, Trekkers. Trekking, Photography, Sightseeing

What Makes It Special

Salaulim Waterfall is a natural attraction located near the Salaulim Dam in Sanguem taluka, South Goa. It is formed by the overflow of water from the Salaulim Dam, especially during the monsoon season, creating a scenic cascade surrounded by greenery.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit is Jul-Oct.

Travel Tip

Follow safety signs and enjoy the viewpoint responsibly.
